From Iberdrola to Sacyr, by way of Cristian Lay or Minits. These are just four of the many customers of Computer Global Software, better known as XOne, a Badajoz-based company that, **for the second year in a row, has been chosen by the American consulting firm Gartner as one of the best in the multiexperience mobile app sector**. In 2020, 16 companies were selected; in 2021 that number fell to 13, but XOne is still in, and is the only Spanish company.
To put this achievement in perspective, Rubén Montero, the general manager, explains that tougher criteria have left out technology giants such as Microsoft and IBM. Gartner, he says, assesses “absolutely everything” about the companies that could enter its ‘magic quadrant’: “They basically **ask you to be at the cutting edge of research, development and innovation**. They check that you have augmented virtual reality and artificial intelligence built in, that you have customers on every continent, that you can provide service in different languages, that you have a minimum number of employees, that you reach a certain revenue…”.
