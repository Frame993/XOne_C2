### Trend 2: a change in the “breakdown/repair” dynamic
> “IoT offers thousands of connected devices capable of self-diagnosis. Today's technology lets us analyze this data and optimize how field technicians are managed.”
The connected devices that make up the Internet of Things allow service companies to adopt proactive strategies. Today, a device can not only monitor itself and anticipate service needs, but also provide a level of detail and context that lets the technician arrive well informed and with the right equipment for the planned job.
Predictive maintenance not only improves customer service relationships; supported by AI and ML, it can also cut costs by optimizing time and anticipating the failure of key components.
This model is gaining ground as connected devices become more ubiquitous and Big Data predicts failures more reliably.
### Trend 3: adapting to new business models
> “Organizations are being forced to change their business models to boost services, which means changing their processes and workloads. In this context, technology is essential to deliver the specific service the customer demands at any given time.”
Service systems were traditionally cost centers based on administrative processes; today they have evolved into profit centers that require organizations to adapt urgently.
More and more companies outsource all their service delivery operations, so the organizations providing them must invest in technological transformation if they want to supply the required information efficiently.
Field services are increasingly part of the overall customer experience, with customers demanding more information and more visibility of their operations. At XOne we offer dashboards, native apps and progressive customer web apps, chatbots… that provide all the information needed.
