XOne has been a technology provider to Iberdrola for more than 10 years, which is why XOne's CEO, Luis Lozano, was invited to the official launch of the **Global Smart Grids Innovation Hub**, a global center of innovation and knowledge that aims to become a **world benchmark in smart grids** and to help meet the challenges of the energy transition.
Over all these years of collaboration with Iberdrola, XOne has carried out many innovative digital projects and solutions, such as a smart dashboard, incident management solutions and remote device control, among many others, contributing its bit to the company's smart and sustainable development.
The center will act as an **innovation platform**, combining the company's technological capabilities with those of its collaborators: suppliers, universities, technology centers and start-ups.
It will also help **attract strategic suppliers and international talent**, strengthening our country's business ecosystem.
The official launch took place in Bilbao on Tuesday, October 5. In addition to presenting the center to the world, the event showcased several **R&D&I projects related to the challenges of the power grids of the future**, including **greater digitalization**, the **processing of the data** these infrastructures generate and how robustly and flexibly the grid responds to new consumption models such as electric mobility and self-consumption, developing new businesses and technology models in distribution networks.
One of the focal points of this new innovation hub will be **collaboration with start-ups** from all over the world. Among other initiatives, the company plans to launch several **challenges** in search of innovative solutions.
Creating this innovative center is Iberdrola's big bet to stay at the **forefront of the energy transition worldwide**.
> “We are very proud to be part of this important ecosystem and to transform the world of energy through technology. That is why at XOne we want to keep moving towards the future, and our goal is to support Iberdrola in its energy transition and sustainability processes, providing solutions that improve the operability and omnichannel reach of its processes. We hope to keep developing smart mobility solutions together, applying disruptive technologies such as artificial intelligence and IoT…”, says Luis Lozano.
