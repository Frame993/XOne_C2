### For your peace of mind: important information first
If you have reached this page, it is because you received a text message from i-DE, as a user of Iberdrola mobility apps, asking you to come here and follow a few steps needed to update your device.
YOU WILL NOT BE ASKED to visit any other website or to install new apps that are not already on your device. You will be **asked to update apps that are already installed and run them** in the work profile of your device.
### Reasons and deadline for the reconfiguration
Due to **infrastructure changes made for cybersecurity reasons**, and so that the apps keep working without problems, all users must carry out the following actions on their devices.
This update **must be completed before November 1**. Otherwise, Control Systems will remove all corporate apps and the device will need to be reinstalled through PACIM.
### Steps to follow on your device
### 1. Update
![Work profile Play Store](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-1.jpg)
Open the Play Store in the work profile of the device, where the i-DE apps are.
![XOne Android MDM in the Play Store](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-2.jpg)
- Find the **XOne Android MDM** app and open it. If the “**UPDATE**” option appears, tap it and update the app. Otherwise, go to the next step.
![XOne Android MDM update button](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-3.jpg)
### 2. Restart
- Once you have checked the previous step, to make sure the latest version of XOne Android MDM is installed, restart the mobile device. **The app must be up to date or the process will not work correctly.**
### 3. Connect
- After restarting the device and checking that you have connectivity (3G, 4G, Wi-Fi…), go back to the **Google Play Store** in the work profile, then find and open the **“Setup Managed”** app.
![Setup Managed app](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-4.jpg)
### 4. Check
- Open the side menu and wait for the green bar shown in the image to appear. If it does not appear, try again after a few minutes.
![Setup Managed side menu](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-5.jpg)
![Green bar showing a correct configuration](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-6.jpg)
### 5. Verify
- Check that you can access the apps you normally use: **eRepos, eGired, Fotos byXOne, LDAP SISCO, mRED, Geoi-De, Replanteos**, etc.
![Corporate apps](/media/Actualizar-Xone-Android-MDM-7.jpg)
### Don't forget to complete these steps
THIS MUST BE DONE ON ALL DEVICES, ESPECIALLY THOSE SWITCHED OFF IN A DRAWER, AND ALWAYS **BEFORE NOVEMBER 1**.
If you have problems with the update, you can open an incident in ITNOW assigned to MEDISTEL, giving the phone number associated with the device, so it can be reviewed.
