In today's technological world, companies that provide technical support must focus on **specialization** and offer **an agile, effective service**.
As a technology partner with broad knowledge and experience in the sector, the **XOne** Multiexperience Development Platform offers the best solution for tracking technical interventions, a proposal that solves the two main challenges we face today: **better service** and **cost savings**.
Below we look at how XOne's field service solution makes managing the whole intervention cycle smoother, ensuring a high-quality, fast, efficient and secure service.
**XOne SAT** is a technical service app that makes it easy to follow up call center requests, manages the specialized team efficiently, quickly identifies the most frequent services and incidents, plans tasks and assigns the right technicians, and shows which products and components have the highest failure rates.
It is a leading, composable and technologically advanced solution that:
- Provides real-time information
- Simplifies data entry in the field
- Shows the team's geolocation
- Is multi-brand and multi-product
- Is based on a single source of data
- Includes AI modules that add value
Some of the features of **XOne SAT** are:
- **360º field service.** Designed specifically for managing a technical service, covering the whole process cycle, from receiving the request to its completion by a technician.
- **Parts requests.** Managed in the field, showing all the options.
- **Stock control.** Up-to-date stock management in warehouses and vehicles.
- **Technician tracking** on the road. See where they are and what they are doing.
- Corporate **instant messaging**: send messages and attachments without going through the office.
- Service **quality surveys.** Linked to incident closure, so technical interventions can be evaluated afterwards.
- **Planning** through configurable mathematical models that determine when and how incoming requests will be handled.
- **Smart assignment** of technicians based on their knowledge and skills, for efficient team management.
- **Route optimizer.** This module plans routes based on assigned incidents, customers and SLAs.
- **Artificial intelligence** to estimate the **mean service life** of installed components, in order to anticipate failures before they happen.
- **Immediate integration.** Customers can be invoiced at the very moment of the repair. With XOne technology the process is standardized so that information flows almost in real time between the different back-end applications and mobility, taking advantage of data captured in the field.
- **Security** for the information handled on the move.
- **Online / offline operation** that fully guarantees work at the customer's site.
- It also includes a field service **KPI module**.
