XOne has been included by Gartner as one of the world's leading low-code providers in its report “Identify and Evaluate Your Next Low-Code Development Technologies”, published on April 13, 2021.
Low-code development is an increasingly proven approach to boosting application developer productivity: it shortens development times and enables composable business strategies, as well as democratized app development for business and citizen developers, while helping companies scale business automation and hyperautomation.
These potential benefits have led to a boom in low-code tools and platforms across every aspect of application development and automation. In response to this new demand, Gartner has tracked more than 400 low-code providers worldwide, encouraging IT leaders to use this research to better understand the landscape of low-code development tools and evaluate the solutions that meet their strategic and tactical needs.
IT leaders face complex, overlapping offerings across multiple low-code development technology markets when they consider acquiring technology.
In this report, Gartner analyzes the advantages of low-code, evaluates the main features and capabilities a technology platform must include as a differentiator, and identifies the world's leading low-code developers and providers, with XOne among the Multiexperience Platforms recommended by the firm.
