The collaborative projects XOne carries out with other organizations, companies and public bodies are a new way of working that responds to an increasingly complex society, in which the only way to compete successfully is through synergies and innovative solutions aligned with the constant changes and advances of the market.
That is why at XOne we are committed to collaborative projects as part of our business strategy, turning them into a competitive advantage for companies, with the aim of inspiring strategic social management.
In this type of project, alliances are forged between different players who share skills and competencies to solve a specific social problem, with initiatives linked to the business strategy that seek financial sustainability.
For this reason, XOne has carried out a collaborative project with Ingeteam called MAS4WIN, “Research into operating conditions and service life in the field of wind energy, using a predictive system based on new digitalization technologies”, a project co-funded with ERDF (FEDER) funds.
Its goal is to promote **technological development**, **innovation and high-quality research**, and to develop a new technological tool and strategy for operation and maintenance applied to activities in the wind energy sector.
Specifically, the main objective of the project is to create a new maintenance concept by extending and estimating service life, as well as researching AR technologies for advanced equipment operation, maintenance and diagnostic services.
This new product will bring to the renewable energy market a solution that significantly improves the quality and reliability of operation and maintenance work on renewable energy systems.
These improvements will lead to a significant increase in the service life of wind sector systems and equipment and, as a result, to lower operating costs, translating into a reduction in the levelized cost of energy (LCOE), the main objective of this project.
In particular, during this research project, specific tools applied to the wind industry will be studied, developed and tested, although the long-term goal is to apply the technology developed to other types of renewable energy plants.
The project has been carried out in cooperation with INGETEAM POWER TECHNOLOGY SA, KAAM INNOVACIÓN Y TECNOLOGÍA, SL., and SERVICIOS Y DESARROLLOS GRUPO VERMON, S.L.
